1. Introduction and Executive Summary

The CashX ecosystem is a suite of four interconnected tokens—CashX, DistroX, DivX, and GSX—supported by an NFT collection and a network of treasuries, all built on PulseChain.

Rather than launching isolated tokens, the ecosystem is designed as a closed economic loop. Every purchase, transaction, and treasury action generates value that flows back into the other components through buy-and-burn pressure, reflections, and revenue sharing.

This document describes the final project in the CashX ecosystem: the GSX token and NFT package. With this launch, the ecosystem is complete. No further tokens or products will be added, and all future effort is directed at growing the components that already exist.

The ecosystem is already capitalized. All five treasuries across the entire ecosystem currently hold a combined total of approximately $110,000 USD, providing a real revenue base from day one.

Four tokens, one economy

Every GSX transaction supports all four tokens simultaneously.

Capitalized treasuries

Five ecosystem treasuries with fixed and transparent allocation rules.

Revenue and deflation

NFT stakers earn uPLS, GSX single stakers earn PLSX, and burns are funded at mint, on transactions, and from treasury operations.

2. The CashX Vision and Mission

Vision. A compact, sustainable token ecosystem where value circulates internally rather than leaking out, and where long-term holders are the primary beneficiaries of growth.

Mission.

  1. Build revenue systems funded by real treasury assets, not emissions alone.
  2. Apply constant, mechanical deflationary pressure to all four tokens.
  3. Keep every allocation rule fixed, published, and verifiable.
  4. Finish building, then focus entirely on compounding what exists.

This is deliberately the last project in the ecosystem. Completion is a feature: instead of diluting attention and liquidity across new launches, the ecosystem shifts to the exponential growth of its existing components.

3. Ecosystem Overview

The ecosystem has five components that feed one another:

  1. CashX. The flagship token, supported by buy-and-burns and reflections funded by NFT mints and GSX activity.
  2. DistroX. Supported by the same buy-and-burn and reflection flows, plus dedicated farms.
  3. DivX. Supported by the same buy-and-burn and reflection flows, plus dedicated farms.
  4. GSX. The engine token. Its 8% transaction tax funds the treasuries, burns, and reflections that power the rest of the ecosystem.
  5. NFTs. The capital formation layer. Minting funds the NFT and GSX treasuries and triggers buy-and-burns of all three legacy tokens.

NFT mints inject capital; GSX activity sustains the loop; and the treasuries convert capital into yield. The result is a circular system: capital enters through NFTs, circulates through GSX transactions and treasuries, and exits as burns that reduce supply and revenue share that rewards holders.

4. Core Mechanics and Tokenomics

CashX, DistroX, and DivX. Each benefits from 333,333 PLS buy-and-burn pressure at every NFT package mint, plus 0.5% GSX transaction buy-and-burn and 0.5% transaction reflections. DistroX and DivX also launch with two farms each.

GSX. Every GSX transaction carries an 8% tax:

  1. 4.0% to the GSX Treasury
  2. 0.5% buy-and-burn for each of CashX, DistroX, DivX, and GSX
  3. 0.5% reflection for each of CashX, DistroX, DivX, and GSX

The GSX Treasury is split into four equal streams: 25% compounds, 25% buys and burns, 25% funds GSX single-stake revenue share in PLSX, and 25% feeds the NFT staking pool in uPLS.

NFT package. Each package is 110 DAI plus 1.5 million PLS and delivers one NFT plus $30 worth of GSX airdropped, valued at 5 PLS per GSX at mint completion. 80 DAI goes to the NFT Treasury; 30 DAI and 500,000 PLS go to the GSX Treasury; and 333,333 PLS each buys and burns CashX, DistroX, and DivX.

5. Revenue Generation and Treasury Systems

Across the ecosystem, five treasuries currently hold approximately $110,000 USD combined. The NFT Treasury is funded by 80 DAI from every NFT mint: 50% compounds and 50% flows to NFT stakers. The GSX Treasury is funded by 30 DAI and 500,000 PLS from every NFT mint, plus 4% of every GSX transaction; it is split into equal compounding, buy-and-burn, PLSX revenue-share, and NFT-staking streams.

Revenue sharing begins 48 hours after launch. Full treasury deployment is expected within two to three weeks, and APR ramps to full throttle over approximately 185 days as the treasuries build principal.

6. Staking, Farms, and Reward Distribution

NFT staking earns uPLS, funded by 50% of NFT Treasury inflows plus 25% of GSX Treasury flows. GSX single staking earns PLSX, funded by 25% of GSX Treasury flows. The project launches two DistroX farms, two DivX farms, and eight to ten GSX farms. Farms distribute ecosystem tokens to participants and deepen liquidity across pairs.

7. Deflationary Mechanisms and Value Flows

Deflation is structural, not discretionary. At mint, each NFT package directs 333,333 PLS to buy and burn each legacy token. On every GSX transaction, 0.5% each buys and burns CashX, DistroX, DivX, and GSX—2% of every transaction permanently removes supply across the four tokens. The GSX Treasury also dedicates 25% to ongoing buy-and-burns.

The intended loop is: NFT mints burn supply and capitalize treasuries; treasuries pay staking rewards; GSX usage taxes 8%, burns more supply, reflects to holders, and refills the treasury; compounding streams grow future rewards; and stronger rewards attract further participation.

8. Roadmap and Key Milestones

  1. Launch: GSX goes live with its 8% tax structure; NFT minting opens; and all farms launch.
  2. Launch + 48 hours: Revenue sharing begins for stakers.
  3. Weeks 1–3: Full treasury deployment is completed.
  4. Days 1–185: APR ramps to full throttle as treasuries build and compound.
  5. PLS 10x trigger: Revenue share across all four coins and the NFTs converts to DAI.
  6. Beyond: No new tokens or products; focus remains on deeper liquidity, larger treasuries, greater burns, and rising revenue share.

9. Conclusion

The CashX ecosystem is intentionally finished: four tokens, one NFT collection, five treasuries, and a fixed set of rules connecting them. Its design rests on circular value flow, sustainable revenue, and structural deflation. With the GSX launch, building is complete; the focus becomes compounding the ecosystem that already exists.
